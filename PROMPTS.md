# Master AI Prompt — South Florida Elevated Motion Hero System

Use this as the base prompt in Google AI Studio / Gemini image generation (or Midjourney), then use the page-specific prompts below.

**Create a premium cinematic hero image for the luxury real estate brand South Florida Elevated — Miguel Perez.**

**BRAND:**
South Florida Elevated
Miguel Perez
Luxury Miami & Fort Lauderdale real estate

**VISUAL IDENTITY:**
Sophisticated South Florida luxury.
Architectural editorial photography.
Ultra-premium real estate campaign.
Modern, cinematic, aspirational, confident, refined and slightly dramatic.

**COLOR SYSTEM:**
• Deep Onyx / near-black
• Charcoal
• Stormy Florida Teal #1F6970
• Florida Teal accents
• Ivory Cream #F4EFE2
• Champagne / Metallic Gold #C9A24A / #D3AF37
• Natural architectural neutrals

**VISUAL LANGUAGE:**
Combine the sophistication of a luxury architectural magazine with the atmosphere of a cinematic South Florida lifestyle campaign.
Think: Miami architecture + Fort Lauderdale waterfront + luxury editorial photography + architectural visualization + fashion-film cinematography.

**IMAGE QUALITY:**
Photorealistic. Extremely detailed. High dynamic range. Natural materials. Realistic reflections. Physically accurate lighting. Premium architectural photography. No artificial-looking CGI. No generic stock-photo appearance.

**COMPOSITION:**
Design specifically as a WEBSITE HERO.
Use strong cinematic depth. Create a clear foreground, middle ground and background. Leave intentional negative space for website typography and CTA overlays. Do NOT place important architectural details directly behind the text area.

**CAMERA:**
Full-frame cinematic camera. 24mm–35mm architectural lens depending on scene. Natural perspective. Subtle depth of field. Controlled highlights. Luxury editorial framing. Low-to-medium camera angle when appropriate.

**MOTION-READY DESIGN:**
The image must be designed to become a subtle cinematic motion hero.
Create visual elements that can move independently:
• foreground architectural elements
• palm leaves
• clouds
• water reflections
• sunlight
• city lights
• curtains
• shadows
• ocean atmosphere
• vehicles in distant background
• subtle human movement where appropriate

Create depth suitable for: slow camera push-in, parallax, horizontal camera drift, foreground/background separation, light sweeps, reflection movement, atmospheric movement.
The final image should feel like the opening frame of a luxury real estate film.

**DO NOT:**
• generic real estate stock photography
• cheesy smiling agents
• excessive HDR
• oversaturated colors
• fake-looking buildings
• distorted architecture
• warped windows
• impossible perspectives
• excessive lens flare
• excessive gold
• cartoon aesthetics
• futuristic sci-fi buildings
• visible AI artifacts
• random text
• fake logos
• watermarks
• random signage
• excessive people
• clutter

**BRAND PRESENCE:**
Keep branding extremely restrained. If a logo is incorporated, use the South Florida Elevated visual identity subtly. Do not make the logo the primary subject.

**OUTPUT:**
Ultra-wide website hero composition. 16:9. 4K-quality visual detail. Premium luxury editorial aesthetic. Designed for desktop and responsive website cropping. Create an image that remains visually powerful when cropped vertically on mobile.

**IMPORTANT:**
Every hero must feel like part of the SAME luxury visual universe while having a distinct subject appropriate to its page. Consistency should come from lighting, color grading, architectural sophistication, composition, depth and cinematic treatment — not from repeating the same scene.
